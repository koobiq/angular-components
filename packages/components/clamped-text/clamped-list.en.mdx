Clamped List displays long lists compactly, hides items that do not fit the configured limit, and lets users expand the full content.

### Clamped list

By default, 10 items are shown and the rest are hidden. If the hidden portion contains fewer than 6 items, the full list is displayed. These parameters can be changed if needed.

### Accessibility

- **The trigger.** The list markup and the trigger are yours to write. `kbqClampedListTrigger` is what makes the trigger accessible: with the directive applied, the trigger is announced as a button, sits in the tab order, points at the list, and reports whether the list is collapsed or expanded. There are no ARIA attributes to write by hand.
- **The label.** The trigger's text is yours to write; the localized "Show more" label with the hidden count, and "Collapse", are there to take from the directive.

The trigger carries no spacing of its own: lay it out with the list.
