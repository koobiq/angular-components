Clamped List displays long lists compactly, hides items that do not fit the configured limit, and lets users expand the full content.

### Clamped list

By default, 10 items are shown and the rest are hidden. If the hidden portion contains fewer than 6 items, the full list is displayed. These parameters can be changed if needed.

### Accessibility

- **The trigger.** The list markup and the trigger are yours to write; `kbqClampedListTrigger` is what makes the trigger accessible. With it, the keyboard reaches the trigger, it is announced as a button, and assistive technology reports whether the list is collapsed or expanded. There are no ARIA attributes to write by hand.
- **The link to the list.** The trigger points at the list, so assistive technology knows what it controls.

The trigger carries no spacing of its own: lay it out with the list.
