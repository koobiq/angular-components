`KbqSkeleton` is a temporary placeholder that occupies the space of the loading element and displays its approximate layout while the actual content is still loading.

### Usage examples

#### Panel loading

Skeletons are also useful when opening a side panel with loading content.

<Example id="skeleton-in-sidepanel" />

#### Button and button group

Put the directive on every button: a button keeps its size and corner radius, and the buttons of a group keep their segments.

<Example id="skeleton-button" />

#### Badge

A badge keeps its size and corner radius.

<Example id="skeleton-badge" />

#### Tag and tag list

Put the directive on every tag: the list keeps its layout and line wrapping.

<Example id="skeleton-tag" />

#### Alert

An alert turns into a single block of its own size, together with the title, icon and text.

<Example id="skeleton-alert" />

#### Description list

Wrap the term and the value in a `span` with the directive: the list keeps its grid, and every line of text gets a bar of its own length.

<Example id="skeleton-dl" />

#### Table

Put the directive on the cell content, not on the cell itself: the table keeps its columns, and the bars follow the length of the text.

<Example id="skeleton-table" />

#### Typography

On an inline element, the skeleton takes the height of the typography level, so headings and body text keep their proportions.

<Example id="skeleton-typography" />

#### List

Put the directive on the text of every option and disable the list while it loads, so that options cannot be selected.

<Example id="skeleton-list" />

#### Tree

Put the directive on the text of every node and on the toggle: the tree keeps its indents. Disable the tree while it loads.

### Guidelines

Use:

- When waiting for page content to load and display, to provide a sense of page structure and minimize layout shifts.
- If the page load takes 2 to 10 seconds, use skeletons.

Don't use:

- If loading takes less than 1 second, no loading indicators are needed.
- If you need to show loading for a single element, such as a video, use a [progress spinner](en/components/progress-spinner). Skeletons are better suited for full-page loading.
- For long-running processes such as file conversion or data upload/download, use [progress bars](en/components/progress-bar) or [progress spinners](en/components/progress-spinner). It's recommended to display the remaining time next to the progress bar.
